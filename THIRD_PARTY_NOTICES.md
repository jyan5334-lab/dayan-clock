DAYAN Clock includes Electron and Chromium. Their licenses and third-party notices
are included in the installed application directory.

UI icons use Lucide, distributed under the ISC license. See `assets/lucide.LICENSE`.

Chinese lunar calendar and almanac calculations use lunar-javascript,
distributed under the MIT license. See `assets/lunar-javascript.LICENSE`.

Twenty background images were generated for this timer and are bundled locally.
The ten PNG wallpapers added in 1.4.1 retain their original 1672x941 pixels;
generation notes and checksums are recorded in assets/backgrounds-20261028.json
and docs/wallpaper-prompts.md. They are not native 4K or 8K images.
The DAYAN application icon was drawn for this project.

The locally bundled 3D island wallpaper uses Three.js r170 (0.170.0), under
its MIT license. See assets/backgrounds/dynamic/derived/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
The user-supplied original scene is preserved with its SHA-256 in
assets/backgrounds/dynamic/originals.json. The derived runtime removes demo UI
and sound and adds the desktop director; it does not fetch CDN scripts.
